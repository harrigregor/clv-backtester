Closing-line value · the only honest edge test

CLV Backtester

Does a model beat the closing line? If yes, there may be a real, durable edge. If no, no staking strategy rescues it. This dashboard reads results/results.json, regenerated automatically each week.

How to read this. The headline metric is average CLV — how much better a price your model's bets got versus the eventual closing line. Positive and sustained across many bets is the rare "edge might be real" signal. ROI on the backtest is secondary and noisier. Calibration tells you whether the model's probabilities can be trusted for stake-sizing: when it says 30%, does it happen 30% of the time? If calibration is off, Kelly sizing will lie to you.

This tool measures whether a predictive edge exists; it does not place bets and makes no guarantees. A positive CLV result is a starting point for further validation across more seasons and leagues — never a green light to bet large. If you ever act on a positive result, size with fractional Kelly (¼ or ½) and hard loss limits, because a backtested edge always looks more certain than it is. Most honest attempts show no edge; that is the expected outcome, and learning it cheaply is the point of this exercise. Gamble only what you can afford to lose.
